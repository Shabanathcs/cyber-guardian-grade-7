MISSION 03

🔗 Link Detective

Scammers build fake websites that look exactly like the real ones. Your only clue is the link (URL). Learn the pro trick, then decide: trust it or not? (+5 XP each)

🕵️ The Pro Trick: Find the REAL Domain

1️⃣Look at the part between https:// and the first single /
2️⃣Read that part from RIGHT to LEFT. The last two words are the real owner, e.g. accounts.google.com
3️⃣Watch for look-alike letters: 0 for o, 1 for l, rn for m
4️⃣🔒 HTTPS only means the connection is private. It does NOT mean the site is honest. Scam sites use HTTPS too.
5️⃣Short links (bit.ly, tinyurl) hide where they really go, so be careful.

🧠 Defender Notes

  • Unique passwords stop one leak from opening all your accounts.
  • 2FA means a stolen password alone is not enough. The hacker also needs the code.
  • Strong passwords are long passphrases, e.g. Sleepy-Camel-Builds-42-Rockets!. Never use your name and birth year.
  • A family-approved password manager can remember all your passwords for you.
  • Never share a 2FA code or OTP. Anyone who asks for it is trying to break into your account.

    MISSION 07

    📰 Fact, Fake or Check First?

    Fake news and AI-made images spread faster than real news. Before you believe or forward something, decide how to treat it. (+5 XP each)

    🔎 The S.I.F.T. Method

    SStop: don't react or forward straight away.
    IInvestigate the source: who posted it? Is it an official account?
    FFind better coverage: are trusted news sites reporting it?
    TTrace to the original: where did the photo or quote first appear?
